Chapter 4 central question: When does a model help ethical perception, and when does it replace ethical perception?

Formal tool: any model, score, label, rule, checklist, category, chart, procedure, or system used to organize information or guide decisions.

3. Formal Domination Audit

Formal Domination: when a label, score, model, rule, or category becomes more powerful than the real person or situation it was supposed to help us understand.

4. Phenomenological Veto Check

Phenomenological Veto: If a model, score, label, or category seriously misdescribes a person’s lived reality, the model should be questioned and revised.
